# Arda 1.0 Personal Agent Ecosystem

## Status and authority

This document is the product-doctrine authority for Arda 1.0. It restores the
complete operator vision after the release plan estate narrowed Arda to
Workbench alone.

Workbench remains a critical and already valuable first-party capability, but
it is not the identity of Arda. Personal Operations, governed proactive
assistance, optional council deliberation, local inference, external-project
work, interoperable systems, economic tools, and multimodal interaction are
composable capabilities of one personal agent ecosystem.

The current release authority is the [0.9 baseline](../releases/0.9/BASELINE.md);
its finite [improvement plan](../archive/2026-08-12-arda-0.9-baseline-and-improvement-plan.md)
is a completed historical record.
The active implementation authority is the segmented
[Ambient Agent Program](../plans/ambient-agent/README.md). The older 1.0
convergence plan is retained as a
[deferred historical record](../archive/deferred/1.0/2026-08-08-arda-1.0-personal-agent-ecosystem-plan.md),
not as competing current authority.
The source-backed gap analysis is
[`../audits/2026-08-08-arda-1.0-vision-to-live-gap-report.md`](../audits/2026-08-08-arda-1.0-vision-to-live-gap-report.md).
Historical Stage 5 evidence and deferred Stage 6 plans retain release-engineering
and future artifact-qualification context, but their older Workbench-only product
boundary no longer limits the meaning of Arda 1.0.

## Executive definition

**Arda is a local-first, phone-accessible, governed digital organism and
personal agent ecosystem that composes small and medium software or hardware
nodes into one coherent adaptive system. It helps an individual organize life,
coordinate work, use hosted and local models, operate external projects,
integrate useful systems, and act proactively without taking unearned
authority.**

Hermes instances, profiles, subagents, model workers, and future specialized
hardware are cognitive cells or organs within that system, not competing
product identities. Arda owns the organism-level continuity: stable identity,
node topology, semantic capability requirements, placement policy, collective
memory, homeostasis, authority, recovery, and cross-node receipts. Standard
Hermes A2A/MCP/API transports should carry those contracts rather than being
reimplemented as proprietary wires.

Arda should reduce the operator's cognitive and administrative burden while
preserving sovereignty, privacy, inspectability, and the ability to stop,
correct, defer, or revoke behavior.

Arda is not defined by any one application, interface, commercial strategy,
model provider, device, or diagnosis.

## Why Arda exists

The operator's life is not divided into independent software silos. Health,
attention, parenting, work, income, creative projects, household routines,
relationships, learning, and infrastructure affect one another. A useful
personal system must maintain continuity across those domains without
flattening them into one optimization target.

The system therefore exists to:

1. preserve context so interrupted work can be resumed;
2. capture thoughts and obligations before they are lost;
3. identify a manageable next action without erasing the larger plan;
4. coordinate capable workers and tools without requiring constant terminal
   supervision;
5. notice meaningful changes and communicate at an appropriate time;
6. protect the operator from unsafe, coercive, wasteful, or falsely confident
   automation;
7. learn from outcomes without silently expanding its authority;
8. allow new capabilities, products, devices, and payment rails to be attached
   when a task requires them;
9. make every consequential action explainable and recoverable;
10. remain valuable even when no task produces income.

## Anti-tunnel-vision doctrine

A recently mentioned concern must not redefine the whole system. Arda applies
capabilities to an objective; it does not turn every objective into the latest
capability discussed.

The following are **optional capabilities**, not universal goals or release
gates:

- revenue generation;
- client delivery;
- Web3 or x402 settlement;
- council debate;
- local inference;
- external project execution;
- phone interaction;
- voice interaction;
- visual or embodied presence;
- wellness-device ingestion;
- environmental sensing.

A task may use none, one, or several of them. Their availability expands the
operator's agency; their presence must not create hidden pressure to invoke
them.

## Human context without product reduction

The system is intended to support an operator managing substantial health,
attention, executive-function, family, and work demands. AuDHD and medication
or health-related cognitive burden make continuity, low-friction capture,
calm reminders, sensory controls, and context recovery especially important.
Those needs shape accessibility and interaction design. They do not turn Arda
into a diagnosis-specific application or medical authority.

Likewise, the need to earn income makes commercial and payment capabilities
important. It does not make personal care, household support, creative work,
or unpaid obligations lower-value tasks.

## Product principles

### 1. One person, one coherent operating context

Personal, business, and system domains remain policy-separated but can be
reasoned about together when the operator authorizes that context. Arda may
recognize conflicts—such as a health routine overlapping a work deadline—while
respecting domain-specific privacy and authority.

### 2. Capability composition, not application silos

Arda's orchestrator selects capabilities according to task requirements:

```text
operator intent or observed condition
  -> context and evidence retrieval
  -> capability requirement classification
  -> governance, Love, and JouleWork assessment
  -> worker/tool/model composition
  -> approval or bounded action
  -> verification and receipts
  -> operator update
  -> governed reflection and learning
```

Applications such as Workbench, Personal Operations, Warden Research,
Mirromere, Company Operations, and future adapters are projections and
capability bundles over the same kernel authorities. They do not create their
own queues, memory authorities, policy systems, identities, or receipt worlds.

### 3. Proactive but not controlling

Arda may observe, summarize, prepare, suggest, remind, and execute narrow
allowlisted actions. It must not use urgency language, repeated interruption,
or inferred personal state to coerce the operator.

Proactivity is bounded by:

- evidence freshness and quality;
- operator-authored preferences and quiet windows;
- interruption and reminder-fatigue budgets;
- reversibility;
- action class and authority requirements;
- Love Dynamics/human-impact review;
- JouleWork burden and resource pressure;
- explicit uncertainty and degraded states;
- durable receipts and revocation paths.

### 4. Language is an action surface

Agent language changes attention, emotion, and behavior. Messages are not
cost-free descriptions. The communications layer must therefore treat wording,
frequency, timing, audience, and confidence as governed effects.

Human-facing output must:

- distinguish fact, inference, suggestion, approval, and action;
- avoid shame, panic, manipulation, and manufactured certainty;
- state why the operator is being interrupted;
- offer deferral or dismissal when safe;
- remain concise by default while preserving inspectable evidence;
- never describe discussion as approval or tests as live acceptance.

### 5. Unknown and unavailable are valid states

Arda must display stale, partial, degraded, unavailable, and failed states. It
must not fill missing runtime, health, council, or worker information with
convincing fiction.

### 6. External capabilities are bounded and removable

Arda should reuse mature external systems through adapters when this is safer
or faster than reimplementation. Connected capabilities do not inherit
authority merely because they are available. Their data, tools, and workers
remain behind Arda contracts, provenance, capability allowlists, and egress
policy.

Hermes is not a disposable subordinate capability in the primary operator
relationship. Hermes owns the live conversational, session, messaging, tool,
skill, delegation, and gateway runtime. Arda surrounds that runtime with
durable intent, Vairë continuity, Varda knowledge governance, risk and approval
policy, device contracts, source-truth projections, and receipts. A bounded
Hermes CLI adapter may still execute isolated jobs, but it cannot define the
system's main interaction architecture.

### 7. Learning does not equal self-authorization

Nightly research and assimilation may collect evidence, compare systems, run
spikes, prepare patches, and recommend adoption. It does not silently copy
code, bypass licensing, mutate protected state, promote every observation into
a task, or merge consequential changes without the required gate.

## Canonical authority model

Arda 1.0 retains one authority per concern:

| Concern | Canonical owner | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Live conversation, sessions, messaging, tools, skills, delegation, and agent execution | Hermes Agent | Arda composes with the primary runtime instead of recreating or transiently wrapping it. |
| Durable objectives, commitments, approvals, and governed physical intents | `arda-core` plus `arda-engine` | Model context and interfaces do not silently replace durable authority. |
| Deterministic service and device execution/recovery | `arda-engine`, systemd, and enrolled outposts | Exact-once observable mutation or explicit compensation; no LLM watchdog. |
| Isolated hosted worker jobs | Hermes adapter | The bounded CLI adapter is optional worker plumbing, not the operator-session architecture. |
| Model/provider routing | Manwë | Routing is optional per task and always receipted. |
| Governance and action classification | `arda-governance` plus policy contracts | Discussion and model confidence are not approval. |
| Memory | Vairë | One canonical store, with personal/business/system domain policy. |
| Communications | Oromë | Platforms are projections and transports, not source of truth. |
| Evidence and observability | Aulë and semantic receipts | Metrics do not replace receipts; UI does not mint truth. |
| Research evaluation | Warden/Varda path | Search preview is not canonical evidence. |
| Lifecycle and audit | HADES/Rúmil bounded surfaces | Audit findings do not authorize mutation. |
| Operator authority | authenticated sovereign operator context | Human-required actions cannot be delegated away. |

## Capability composition contract

Every orchestrated objective must be expressible as a capability request rather
than a hard-coded application workflow. The future
`arda.capability-composition.v1` contract must include:

- objective and stable lineage IDs;
- requested outcome and acceptance conditions;
- relevant project and memory scopes;
- data sensitivity and allowed egress;
- required and optional capabilities;
- worker roles rather than fixed model names;
- hosted/local/offline route preferences;
- council policy: disabled, critic-only, adjudication, or full deliberation;
- proactive communication policy;
- budget across time, compute, network, storage, attention, and reasoning;
- authority and approval classes;
- tool, filesystem, network, and payment permissions;
- verification commands or other acceptance evidence;
- restart, cancellation, and compensation behavior;
- completion and operator-notification policy.

The planner may choose among capabilities, but it may not add a capability that
violates the request's sensitivity, authority, budget, or explicit exclusions.

## Love, JouleWork, and governance

### Love Dynamics and the legacy Love Equation

The repository currently contains two related but distinct concepts:

1. `arda-governance::love_equation` is explicitly documented in source as a
   **legacy task-value compatibility proxy** using impact, reach, energy, and
   time. It must not be presented as canonical relational alignment.
2. Canonical cooperation, defection, relationship, and human-impact reasoning
   belongs to the newer Love Dynamics surface.

Arda 1.0 must preserve this distinction in contracts and UI. The legacy proxy
may inform value-efficiency estimates. Human-facing, relational, care, consent,
and coercion concerns require canonical Love Dynamics plus policy—not a single
numeric threshold.

Required human-impact questions include:

- Who benefits and who bears the burden?
- Is interruption justified now?
- Is the action reversible?
- Does the language preserve dignity and agency?
- Is an inferred personal state being treated as fact?
- Could optimizing one domain harm health, relationships, privacy, or future
  autonomy?

### JouleWork

JouleWork measures more than financial cost. The live economics surface
already models compute, network, storage, attention, and reasoning units and
tracks measurement provenance and confidence.

Arda 1.0 must extend operational use so that planning and review account for:

- operator attention and interruption burden;
- hosted-model cost and token usage;
- local compute, energy, thermal, and availability pressure;
- network and storage use;
- elapsed time and recovery work;
- estimated-versus-observed honesty;
- uncertainty when fallback estimates are used.

Default-fallback measurements cannot be treated as autonomy truth. Synthetic
restore totals remain continuity estimates, not audit-grade event evidence.

### Action classes

A practical authority ladder is:

| Class | Example | Default behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Observe | read health, task, service, or project state | act locally and receipt if persisted |
| Organize | classify capture, prepare context, rank candidate actions | reversible local action; disclose inference |
| Inform | concise status or calm reminder | apply timing, fatigue, and language policy |
| Propose | plan, patch, draft message, adoption recommendation | no external side effect |
| Execute reversible | allowlisted local action with compensation | policy gate and receipt; approval based on scope |
| Execute consequential | external message, production deploy, payment, retention change | explicit scoped approval or stronger policy |
| Prohibited | covert surveillance, diagnosis, medication change, silent authority expansion | fail closed |

Council deliberation, local-model votes, and model confidence may contribute
evidence but cannot lower the required authority class.

## Orchestration and workers

### Hermes/Arandur orchestrator responsibility

The primary orchestrator runs through Hermes while consuming Arda continuity,
governance, and capability contracts. It should:

- understand intent and current life/project context;
- decide whether to ask, act, defer, or monitor;
- compose the smallest capable worker set;
- avoid debate when it adds no value;
- use local resources where appropriate without sacrificing correctness;
- keep the operator informed at meaningful boundaries;
- stop or re-plan when evidence invalidates the current path;
- preserve receipts and a restart-safe state;
- learn from outcomes without converting correlation into authority.

### Hosted powerful model

A strong hosted model is appropriate for:

- difficult coding and architecture;
- ambiguous planning;
- cross-domain synthesis;
- final adjudication after meaningful disagreement;
- tasks whose failure cost exceeds model cost.

### Local inference fleet

Local models should be usable for bounded roles such as:

- source and session summarization;
- independent requirement review;
- security, privacy, or failure critique;
- classification and routing suggestions;
- draft comparison;
- low-risk council notes;
- offline continuity.

Local models must not be kept idle merely because hosted inference is the main
coding lane. They also must not be invoked ceremonially when deterministic
code or one model is sufficient.

### Council protocol

Council is an optional execution protocol:

1. proposer produces a plan or recommendation;
2. selected critics independently review assigned concerns;
3. disagreements are persisted with evidence and confidence;
4. an adjudicator resolves only material tensions;
5. governance determines whether approval is required;
6. execution proceeds or the issue returns to the operator.

A useful council output contains seat opinions, agreement, tension,
uncertainty, synthesis, and escalation recommendation. It does not manufacture
consensus or approval.

## Interaction surfaces

All interaction surfaces address the same projects, runs, tasks, memories,
receipts, and sessions.

| Surface | Primary role | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Phone through Hermes Gateway | ubiquitous capture, status, approvals, concise results | an isolated task universe |
| Desktop/TUI | deep engineering and inspection | the only usable control path |
| HUD | spatial awareness, intervention, agent canvases, native workstations | a web dashboard or backend authority |
| Voice | low-friction capture and dialogue | irreversible action without review |
| Mirromere | calm ambient presence and personal routine projection | surveillance or medical authority |
| Relic/CITADEL | embodied runtime-truth projection | decorative fiction or execution authority |
| Discord/Matrix/Telegram | transport and social projection | canonical council or task storage |
| MCP/API/tools | interoperable programmatic access | a policy bypass |

Phone accessibility is a required 1.0 operator path, but loss of phone
connectivity must not stop local execution, recovery, or desktop access.

## Personal Operations

Personal Operations is a first-party capability bundle over the same kernel.
It provides:

- immediate capture without forced categorization;
- inbox and review-assisted organization;
- schedules and bounded reminders;
- a “what was I doing?” context resume card;
- morning and transition briefs with sources and uncertainty;
- personal data export and deletion boundaries;
- optional voice capture;
- future validated-device wellness adapters after privacy gates.

Personal Operations must keep personal records scoped and prevent unrelated
business, council, or infrastructure workers from receiving sensitive content.

## Proactive assistance

A proactive cycle must be event- and policy-driven, not an unrestricted timer
that invents work:

```text
observe change
  -> correlate with active objectives and operator preferences
  -> classify urgency, freshness, and uncertainty
  -> estimate JouleWork and interruption burden
  -> apply Love/human-impact and action-class policy
  -> choose silent record, digest, notification, proposal, or allowed action
  -> persist outcome and next evaluation time
```

Examples of acceptable proactive behavior:

- noticing an overdue operator-authored routine and sending one calm reminder;
- detecting a failed run and preparing a concise recovery choice;
- creating a context-resume packet before a scheduled work block;
- identifying that a local model can perform an overnight review;
- warning that a proposed task exceeds its attention or compute budget;
- preparing an external-system adoption proposal after a measured need appears.

Examples of prohibited or gated behavior:

- inferring a diagnosis from communication patterns;
- repeatedly escalating a dismissed reminder;
- moving money, sending commitments, or changing medical information without
  scoped authority;
- turning every note or research observation into a task;
- silently rewriting code because an external project looked useful.

## External projects and imported systems

### External projects

A project contract allows Arda to operate a repository or other bounded
workspace without absorbing it into Arda. External projects retain their own
commands, artifacts, acceptance criteria, and version history. Arda supplies
orchestration, workers, policy, evidence, recovery, and operator visibility.

### Adapter-first adoption

External systems enter through a supervised adapter with:

- identity, version, source, license, digest, and SBOM;
- declared network/filesystem/process capabilities;
- secret references and egress policy;
- input/output schemas and limits;
- timeout, cancellation, retry, and idempotency behavior;
- health, freshness, and degraded states;
- receipt correlation;
- backup, removal, and compatibility behavior.

### Governed nightly assimilation

The nightly loop may:

1. discover or receive a candidate;
2. collect canonical source and license evidence;
3. compare it with an existing measured need;
4. run the candidate in an isolated fixture;
5. record which capabilities are actually used;
6. prepare a clean-room implementation or bounded patch;
7. run tests, provenance checks, and removal checks;
8. emit an adoption/assimilation proposal;
9. auto-land only pre-authorized, low-risk, reversible changes;
10. require approval for dependencies, licensing, architecture, private data,
    network egress, or consequential behavior.

This is **use first, measure, then assimilate**—not automated code harvesting.

## Optional economic capabilities

Commercial and Web3 capabilities are attachable when an objective requires
them. They can support:

- external client projects;
- proposals, quotes, invoices, and delivery receipts;
- paid APIs or agent services;
- conventional payment processors;
- wallet or x402 settlement;
- economic discovery and machine-to-machine purchasing.

Economic capabilities must declare:

- payer/payee and asset/network identity;
- quote and expiry;
- maximum amount and cumulative budget;
- deliverable or service-level acceptance condition;
- replay protection and idempotency;
- refund/dispute or compensation path;
- tax/accounting export requirements;
- explicit operator authority for funds movement;
- receipts linking payment to the originating run.

Revenue is never a default objective. No personal task becomes lower priority
because it cannot be monetized.

## Health and blood-pressure device boundary

Arda may support validated-device ingestion as an optional Personal Operations
capability after privacy approval. The initial Wi-Fi blood-pressure integration
must identify the actual device model and supported local, Home Assistant,
standards, export, or vendor API path.

Required measurement fields include:

- device and adapter identity;
- source and collection timestamps;
- systolic, diastolic, pulse, and units when supplied;
- evidence class and measurement protocol;
- duplicate, stale, malformed, and disagreement state;
- operator correction metadata;
- retention and export policy;
- provenance receipt.

Required boundaries:

- validated cuff readings remain distinct from manually entered or derived
  estimates;
- no diagnosis, dosage change, treatment recommendation, or silent emergency
  authority;
- no council or unrelated worker broadcast;
- no cloud egress without explicit adapter policy;
- trend summaries expose source quality and uncertainty;
- alert thresholds come from an explicit operator/clinician-authored protocol,
  not agent improvisation;
- device loss or network failure produces an unavailable state, not fabricated
  continuity.

## Memory and continuity

Vairë remains the sole canonical memory system. Primary domains are:

- `personal` — operator-authored context, routines, personal operations, and
  sensitive wellness records;
- `business` — projects, decisions, outcomes, commitments, and economic work;
- `system` — runtime health, routing, recovery signatures, and infrastructure.

Subsystem scopes remain separate metadata. Personal recall requires authorized
consumer context. Health records use the strictest path and are not promoted
from inferred behavior.

Arda must preserve:

- correction and supersession chains;
- revocation and quarantine;
- retention and bounded compression;
- source confidence and provenance;
- explicit stale or unavailable projections;
- restart-safe context resumption;
- operator export and deletion rights consistent with audit obligations.

## Truth and completion vocabulary

No document, UI, or agent may use “complete,” “finished,” “working,” or
“operational” without naming the achieved level.

| Level | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Specified | Versioned design or contract exists. |
| Implemented | Source exists and focused tests pass. |
| Compile-active | Supported feature/profile includes the source. |
| Root-composed | Canonical runtime constructs or supervises it. |
| Operator-reachable | Supported phone, CLI, API, launcher, or HUD path reaches it. |
| Workflow-proven | A real end-to-end path exercises it with receipts. |
| Failure-proven | Restart, cancellation, malformed state, timeout, and degraded dependencies are exercised. |
| Operator-accepted | The operator uses the intended interface and accepts the behavior. |
| Release-supported | Artifact, install, compatibility, docs, diagnostics, privacy, and support gates pass. |

Task-specific evidence such as `phone_access_verified`,
`local_inference_verified`, `council_deliberation_verified`,
`payment_verified`, or `device_ingestion_verified` is attached only when the
objective requires that capability.

Unit tests and schemas never substitute for live or operator acceptance.
Discussion never substitutes for approval. A worker's self-report never
substitutes for independent artifact or runtime evidence.

## Arda 1.0 product promise

Arda 1.0 should let the operator:

1. reach the same personal system from phone and desktop;
2. capture personal or project intent without losing context;
3. see what is active, blocked, stale, or awaiting authority;
4. attach a personal, software, research, household, or commercial objective;
5. have the orchestrator compose appropriate workers and capabilities;
6. converse, delegate, use tools, and continue sessions through Hermes as the
   primary agent runtime;
7. use local inference for useful roles when appropriate;
8. invoke council deliberation only when warranted;
9. receive proactive, calm, evidence-backed messages;
10. approve or reject consequential actions remotely;
11. recover runs and context after restart;
12. integrate external projects and adapter-based systems without surrendering
    authority;
13. preserve private personal memory with scoped recall;
14. attach optional economic or device capabilities without making them system
    defaults;
15. inspect evidence and correct the system's understanding.

## 1.0 compatibility foundation versus optional distributions

The 1.0 kernel and contracts must support the entire ecosystem, but not every
peripheral must ship as a required component.

### Required for the Arda 1.0 base

- Hermes-centered conversation, session, tool, delegation, and gateway runtime;
- Arda-owned durable intent, governance, continuity, and receipt contracts;
- capability composition contract;
- bounded worker execution and verification;
- phone-accessible operator session with shared lineage;
- Personal Operations capture, resume, reminders, and briefs;
- Vairë memory domain policy;
- proactive policy cycle with quiet/fatigue controls;
- optional council and local-inference worker roles;
- adapter and external-project boundaries;
- truthful HUD/CLI/phone projections;
- governance, Love Dynamics, JouleWork, receipts, restart, and recovery;
- no-false-completion acceptance suite.

### Optional 1.0 capabilities or private-alpha distributions

- x402/payment execution;
- Company Operations and client workflow projections;
- validated blood-pressure device adapter;
- ambient voice dialogue;
- Mirromere hardware;
- Relic/CITADEL presence expansion;
- Home Assistant and other external sidecars;
- camera or environmental sensing.

Optional means disabled or absent without degrading the core. It does not mean
unimportant or abandoned. The 1.0 contracts must permit these capabilities to
attach without creating a new authority system.

## Final doctrine

Arda succeeds when it expands an individual's practical agency while reducing
the burden of remembering, coordinating, checking, and recovering work.

It should be capable enough to organize a life, complete difficult projects,
make use of available models and machines, integrate new systems, and support
income when requested. It should also be restrained enough to preserve the
operator's privacy, attention, relationships, health boundaries, and final
judgment.

The point of governance is not to make the system inert. The point is to make
proactivity, learning, and power safe enough to become genuinely useful.
