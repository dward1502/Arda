## Evidence classification lanes

Use these lanes before any future promotion, public text, experiment plan, or canonical architecture reference.

| Lane | Definition | Current examples | Promotion rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source inventory | File paths, hashes, extraction status, and provenance facts. | Phase 2F source list; existing source-extraction provenance. | May be used as audit/provenance when hashes and paths are verified. |
| External source pointer | References to papers, studies, tools, places, or named methods that need independent retrieval. | arXiv `2601.14096`; HeartMath/cardiac coherence; MacDougall mass-loss experiment; Whaley House; stone tape theory; SQUID magnetometer references. | Must be checked against primary sources before being treated as evidence. |
| Research hypothesis | A falsifiable or partly falsifiable proposal. | ECST as electromagnetic consciousness synthesis; passive multi-sensor triangulation; MythOS embedding-space/error-minimization bridge. | May become a research question or protocol, not a truth claim. |
| Interpretive synthesis | AI-assisted or author synthesis connecting multiple sources. | Claims that validated components imply ECST; mapping embedding-space theory onto MythOS architecture. | Must be labeled as synthesis and reviewed for unsupported leaps. |
| Anecdote / lived report | Personal or local observation. | Whaley House apparition/smell reports; transplant-derived sensitivity framing. | May motivate hypotheses only; not evidence by itself. |
| Measurement protocol idea | Proposed study design or instrumentation. | Three-point sensor array; EMF/infrasound/full-spectrum/temperature/audio correlation; AI anomaly filtering. | Requires pre-registration, controls, instrument specs, and safety/privacy review before execution. |
| Architecture analogy | Use of research as inspiration for Annunimas/MythOS/Oracle design. | Council geometry, embedding-space navigation, error minimization, substrate-independent cognition. | May inform design only when decoupled from scientific validation claims. |
| Canonical claim | A statement asserted as established fact. | None approved by this gate. | Blocked until citation, contradiction, and human review are complete. |

## Claim-handling rules

1. Do not state that ECST, soul-current persistence, plasmoid carrier mechanisms, hauntings, or 432 Hz signatures are established facts.
2. Do not treat Claude/AI transcript language such as "confirmed" or probability estimates as independent scientific verification.
3. Do not use personal experience, transplant anecdotes, or place lore as more than hypothesis-generating context.
4. Keep scientific, biomedical, spiritual, paranormal, and architectural claims in separate lanes.
5. Require source retrieval, citation review, contradiction review, and human review before any public-facing or canonical claim.
6. Separate platform architecture value from scientific truth value: MythOS/Oracle can use error-minimization and multi-perspective validation patterns without requiring ECST to be true.

## Safe next research tasks

- Extract binary DOCX/PDF sources into derived audit artifacts with source hash, extracted text hash, tool/version, and timestamp.
- Build a claim inventory table with columns for claim text, source path, lane, external citation needed, contradiction check needed, confidence, and promotion status.
- Retrieve and summarize external sources before attaching them as evidence.
- Draft a measurement-protocol outline only as a proposed study, with controls and negative cases clearly listed.
- Keep all outputs private/review-gated until explicit human review promotes them.

## Exit criteria for future promotion

A future gate may promote individual claims only when it has:

- verified source provenance,
- primary-source citations,
- contradiction and alternative-explanation review,
- confidence labels per claim,
- separation of hypothesis/protocol/anecdote/architecture lanes,
- explicit human approval for any public or canonical use.
