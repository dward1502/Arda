## 

Why Claude + Suno beats Suno alone.

Suno generates music from text prompts. The quality of the output depends entirely on the quality of the prompt. Most people type "happy pop song" and get generic garbage. The top 1% of earners think about prompts differently.

This is exactly where Claude comes in.

A bad Suno prompt:

> Calm music for videos

A Claude-optimized Suno prompt:

php-template

```text
Soft acoustic guitar with gentle piano melody, morning coffee 
atmosphere, no drums or percussion, warm analog tape sound, 
90 seconds, perfect for YouTube background, ends with gentle 
fade, no lyrics, tempo 72 BPM, key of G major, production 
quality similar to lo-fi but cleaner, emotional warmth
```

The difference in output quality is not subtle. It's the difference between a track that gets rejected by AudioJungle and one that sells 40 times.

The Claude prompt that writes your Suno prompts:

yaml

```yaml
You are an expert music producer who specializes in writing 
prompts for Suno AI music generator.

I want to create a catalog of [genre] tracks for [use case].
Target platform: [AudioJungle / YouTube / Spotify / Fiverr]

For each track idea, write a detailed Suno prompt that includes:
- Specific instruments and their role (lead, rhythm, background)
- Exact tempo in BPM
- Key and scale
- Mood descriptors (3-5 specific emotional words)
- Production style reference (lo-fi, cinematic, warm, etc.)
- Duration and structure (intro, build, drop if applicable)
- What to exclude (no drums / no lyrics / no distortion)
- Platform-specific requirements

Generate 10 different prompt variations that would create
a cohesive but varied catalog. Each track should feel 
related but distinct.
```
