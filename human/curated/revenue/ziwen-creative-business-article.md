## 

How to start your account (Warm-up & Logic)

If you ignore this chapter, your M4 Mac Mini is just an expensive heater. Platforms like TikTok, IG, and YouTube now utilize Environmental Fingerprinting. They know if an account exists only to post.

To bridge the gap between "Bot" and "Human," we use a Manual Warm-up. For the first week, Ada stays in the hangar. You are the pilot.

1. The 7-Day Warm Up Phase

For the first 7 days, zero automated posting. Your goal is to build Trust Signals that a script simply cannot mimic perfectly.

Days 1–3: The "Consumer" Persona

- Logins: Log in manually on your primary device 2–3 times a day.
    
- The Feed: Spend 30 minutes scrolling the "For You" or "Home" page.
    
- Retention: Watch videos in your niche to the end. This tells the algorithm, "This user likes this content, so we should show their future posts to people like them."
    
- Signals: Like 3–5 videos and leave 1–2 organic comments. Be a real person.
    

Days 4–7: The "Active Member" Persona

- Networking: Follow 3–5 top-tier creators from your channels.json list.
    
- High-Value Engagement: Leave one thoughtful comment on a trending video in your niche daily. Pro-Tip: Don't just say "Cool video." Disagree with a point, ask a deep question, or add a tip.
    
- Search Discovery: Use the search bar to look up keywords related to your startup or niche.
    

2. Style Cloning (The Local Advantage)

While you're doing your manual 15-minute sessions, don't just scroll aimlessly. Use this time to calibrate the "vision" for your brand. Treat every "Like" as a vote for the future of your content.

Mental Blueprinting

As you scroll, keep a mental checklist (or a quick note for Ada) of the specific elements you want to replicate. Ask yourself:

- The Hook: What made me stop scrolling? Was it the text on screen, the first sentence, or a specific visual?
    
- The Vibe: Does this feel high-end and minimalist (Vercel-style), or is it loud and chaotic?
    
- The Utility: Am I actually learning something, or is this just "fast food" content?
    

Creating "Unique-to-You" DNA

Because you are keeping your specific style in mind, your local clipping service won't look like a template.

- Custom Themes: You’ll notice a specific color grade or font choice you love.
    
- Unique Pacing: You’ll realize you prefer 2-second cuts over 0.5-second "seizure-inducing" edits.
    
- Signature Captions: You can instruct Ada to build a caption style that is yours and yours alone.
    

3. The First Post (The Soft Launch)

On Day 8, we move our first clip from the /final folder to

[Postiz](https://postiz.pro/ziwen-xu)

.

- The Rule of One: Only post one video.
    
- No External Links: Do not put a link in your bio or the description yet. External links on a fresh account are a massive "Bot Flag."
    
- The Seed: Use your "Supporter" accounts to watch the video twice and leave a question. This tells the algorithm, "Hey, people are actually talking about this."
    

4. Scaling the "Pulse"

Once the first few videos get more than 1000 views constantly, Ada increases her "Pulse."

- Week 2: 1 post/day + 20 mins engagement.
    
- Week 3: 2 posts/day + 15 mins engagement.
    
- Steady State: 3 posts/day. Never exceed 4. In 2026, "High Volume" is often penalized as "Low Quality."

The Evolution (The Analyst Loop)

Most "AI automation" is just a hamster wheel, it follows the same script forever, regardless of the results. If a video gets 0 views, a dumb bot will just post another one exactly like it.

Ada is different. She uses a self-correcting loop inspired by Karpathy’s AutoResearch. She doesn't just post content; she researches what makes content succeed and physically rewrites her own strategy via

[evolver.py](https://evolver.py/)

which is within

[Social Media Autoresearch](https://clawhub.ai/duolahypercho/social-media-autoresearch)

1. The Verdict System (KEEP or KILL)

The evolver script acts as the "Senior Editor" of your media house. After an experiment runs (e.g., testing a new caption style or a different hook logic), Ada looks at the data and executes a verdict:

- KEEP: If the experiment score (views/retention) beats the current "Champion Baseline," the change is integrated permanently. The baseline.json is updated, and this new style becomes the standard.
    
- KILL: If the change underperformed, Ada triggers the Kill Protocol. She discards the mutation and reverts all files to their original state using the /backups folder.
    

2. The "Circuit Breaker" (Safety First)

One of the most "legit" features of this script is the Kill Streak protection.

- If Ada fails three experiments in a row (3 consecutive KILLs), the evolved script triggers a Circuit Breaker.
    
- It pauses all new experiment proposals and demands a Human Review. This prevents the agent from spiraling into a "hallucination loop" where it keeps trying bad ideas.
    

3. The baseline.json (The Agent’s DNA)

Every successful experiment updates the baseline.json. This file tracks the evolution of your agent’s "Strategy."

- Version Control: You can see your agent grow from v1 (Generic) to v50 (Highly Optimized Viral Specialist).
    
- Changelog: Every "KEEP" verdict is logged with its ID and the percentage increase in performance (e.g., +12.5% retention).
